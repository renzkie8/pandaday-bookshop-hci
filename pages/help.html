Order & Delivery

Payment Methods
GCash, Maya, Cash on Pickup

Q: What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash on Pickup, GCash, and Maya. E-wallet payments must be confirmed before your order is processed.

Q: Can I pay upon pickup?

Yes! Select "Cash on Pickup" during checkout and pay when you collect your items at your chosen branch.

Returns & Refunds
Return policy, exchange procedure

Q: What is the return policy?

Items may be returned within 7 days of purchase with original receipt. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Visit any branch to process a return or exchange.

Kaibigan Card
Membership, points, rewards

Q: How do I earn points?

Every ₱10 spent = 1 Kaibigan point. Points can be redeemed for discounts (100 pts = ₱10 off).

Q: How do I apply for a Kaibigan Card?

Visit any Pandaday branch or register online through your Account page. It's free!
